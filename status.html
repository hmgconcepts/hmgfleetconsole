🔧 Scheduled maintenance windows

Planned downtime must never alarm: during a window this console logs one quiet info line instead of red incidents, suppresses desktop/webhook alerts for that project, and shows a blue 🔧 tile on the wallboard. Keep-alive pings continue regardless — Supabase's 7-day inactivity clock does not respect maintenance plans.
