Where the Fleet Console fits

Every HMG product deployment is a client-owned Supabase + Vercel stack: the school/church/practice owns its accounts, its data and its site. That independence is the point — but it also means dozens of separate free-tier projects, each with Supabase's 7-day inactivity pause hanging over it, each with its own subscription cycle, each capable of failing in its own way.

The Fleet Console is HMG Technologies' answer: one internal screen that watches them all — keep-alive writes so no project ever pauses, five-probe health checks, an automatic incident journal, renewal tracking, printable SLA reports and a wallboard for the office. It monitors infrastructure, never data: only public anon keys are stored, and Row-Level Security on every client database keeps students, fees, results and members unreadable by construction.

Built by HMG Technologies (software engineering arm of HMG CONCEPTS) with the same rules as everything we ship: free tools only, no paid AI APIs, no tracking, and the client's interests first.
